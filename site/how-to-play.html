How to Play

Everything you need to go from first word to all-time legend.

▶  Enter the Arena

🎮 The Basics

Champ Words is a real-time multiplayer word race. A word is shown to the room, and everyone types their guess as fast as they can. The first correct guess wins the most points; slower correct guesses still score, just less. Wrong answers cost nothing but precious seconds.

  • Rooms: create a room and share the 4-letter code, or join the active arena — up to 8 players per game.
  • Rounds: a fresh word drops every round. The round ends when the word is found or the timer hits zero.
  • Speed rounds: every 5th round is a 15-second sprint with 3× points on the line.

⭐ Scoring

  • Fastest correct answer — 200 points (flat, Skribble-style).
  • Later correct answers — points scale down with time left.
  • Streak bonus — +50 points on every correct answer after a streak of 2+.
  • Speed rounds — all points multiplied by 3.
  • Duel wins — 100 points per victory.
  • Milestones — 100+ points in a single round unlocks the “Century Round” achievement and more.

🧩 Power-Ups & Commands

Type these in the answer box or in-game chat. Power-ups cost points — spend wisely.

!hint — 50 pts

Reveal one letter of the word. Great when you're one letter away from glory.

!freeze — 100 pts

Add 5 seconds to the round timer. Can turn a lost round into a comeback.

!challenge — 100 pts

Duel the round winner — 30 seconds, first correct answer takes the 100-point prize.

!score — free

See your own score card at any moment (15-second cooldown).

💡 Pro Tips

  • Watch for the 40-second mark — two letters get revealed automatically to keep the round alive.
  • Keep a streak alive: every answer after 2+ in a row pays a bonus, and streaks feed your XP and level.
  • Hoard points early rounds so you can afford Hints and Freezes when the word is brutal.
  • Speed rounds are where boards flip — save your Freeze for those 15-second sprints.
  • Use voice chat to divide and conquer: one player guesses while another watches the clock.

🏆 Leaderboards & Achievements

The all-time leaderboard keeps your lifetime score forever. Today's race resets at midnight, so every day is a fresh chance to be crowned. Earn achievements like “Speed Demon” (solve in under 5 seconds), “Century Round” and more — they're shown right on your player profile.
