Champ Words — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with TikTok, we access your TikTok public profile information, including:

  • Your display name
  • Your profile avatar
  • Your TikTok user ID

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information solely to:

  • Identify you when you play the Game
  • Display your name and avatar to other players in your game room
  • Track your scores and progress

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law.

4. Data Storage

Your session information is stored temporarily on the Game server for the duration of your login session (24 hours maximum).

5. TikTok's Privacy

Your use of TikTok Login is also governed by TikTok's own terms and privacy policy. Please review them at tiktok.com.

6. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Game administrator.
